# Corpus Analysis

The corpus analysis should combine corpus linguistics with argument/claim annotation.

## Exploratory methods

- frequency and dispersion;
- concordance analysis;
- collocation and lexical association;
- definitional/boundary language detection;
- temporal and disciplinary variation;
- distribution of classification and normative claims.

## Confirmatory methods

After pilot annotation and model selection, specify the variables and statistical tests before running the held-out confirmatory corpus.

## CADS principle

Corpus-assisted discourse analysis can identify recurrent lexical and discourse patterns without requiring the analyst to preselect confirming passages. Qualitative close reading remains necessary for interpreting argument structure, but candidate passages should be sampled according to transparent rules.

## Automation

LLM or NLP systems may assist retrieval, clustering, and preliminary extraction. Human annotation and reliability testing are required for claims that depend on semantic or argumentative interpretation.
