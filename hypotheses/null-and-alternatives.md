# Null and Alternative Hypotheses

## H0 — No special normative compression

Observed reductions in argumentative distance are fully explained by ordinary discourse compression and do not depend on normative classification.

## H2 — Descriptive compression

The same pattern appears in classifications with little or no normative significance.

## H3 — Genre confounding

Compression is primarily a property of genre or discipline rather than of conceptual engineering.

## H4 — Advocacy confounding

Compression is primarily associated with advocacy writing and is not distinctive of conceptual engineering.

## H5 — Term-specificity

The effect is strong for Indigenous but weak or absent for comparison concepts.

## H6 — Symmetry

Comparable compression occurs when competing political projects use the same or analogous conceptual strategies.

## H7 — Boundary-policing alternative

Boundary exclusions are better explained by ordinary definitional maintenance than by preservation of a downstream normative inference.

A result supporting H1 is most informative if it discriminates among these alternatives rather than merely finding a nonzero association.
