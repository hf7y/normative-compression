# Primary Hypothesis

## H1 — Normative compression

When a classificatory concept carries normative implications, discourse will exhibit measurable reductions in explicit argumentative distance between empirical/descriptive claims and normative conclusions relative to appropriate controls.

### Stronger mechanism hypothesis

Boundary-policing moves will be more frequent when admitting an adversarial case would weaken the normative inference associated with the category.

### Observable predictions

1. Classification nodes will have higher centrality in arguments ending in normative conclusions than in matched descriptive arguments.
2. Normative premises will be less frequently explicit when the classifier has a strongly normative inferential profile.
3. Boundary disputes will predict preservation of downstream normative inferences.
4. These effects will remain after controlling for genre, document length, discipline, publication period, and advocacy status, if those controls are available.

These are provisional predictions. The final specification must be frozen after pilot work and before confirmatory analysis.
