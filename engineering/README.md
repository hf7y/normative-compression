# Engineering Theory

This layer asks what it would mean to treat conceptual engineering as engineering in a strong sense.

## Objects

- concepts
- classifiers
- inferential profiles
- conceptual networks
- codebooks / vocabularies
- boundary criteria
- argumentative states

## Operations to investigate

- encode
- compress
- expand / decompress
- filter
- reclassify
- boundary-police
- error-correct
- transmit
- stabilize
- replace

These are hypotheses about useful analytical categories, not assumptions that all conceptual activity literally instantiates them.

## Engineering schema

A mature engineering theory should specify:

1. objects;
2. representations;
3. operations;
4. target states;
5. constraints;
6. models;
7. interventions;
8. performance criteria;
9. measurements;
10. failure modes;
11. feedback mechanisms.

## Central question

> Can conceptual engineering be described as a theory of representational transformation with explicit operations, constraints, and measurable outcomes?

The answer must be established empirically and formally rather than inferred from the usefulness of engineering metaphors.
