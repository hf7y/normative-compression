# Inclusion and Exclusion

This file will contain the frozen corpus criteria once the literature and source landscape have been surveyed.

## Initial principles

Include documents that:

- fall within a defined source population;
- meet the publication/genre/date criteria for their stratum;
- contain analyzable uses of the target classificatory concepts.

Exclude only for documented, pre-specified reasons such as:

- inaccessible/non-textual content;
- duplicate versions;
- insufficient metadata;
- outside the temporal/source frame;
- inability to establish provenance.

Do not exclude a document because its argument is inconvenient to the hypothesis.

## Versioning

Any change to inclusion/exclusion rules after corpus inspection must be logged in `docs/decision-log.md` and, for confirmatory work, treated as a new specification.
