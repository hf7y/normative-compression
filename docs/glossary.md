# Glossary

## Conceptual engineering
Deliberate evaluation, revision, introduction, stabilization, or replacement of representational resources for specified purposes.

## Normative compression
Proposed phenomenon in which normative information needed for an inference is carried by a classificatory representation rather than stated as an independent premise.

## Inferential profile
The set of inferences a concept conventionally supports, licenses, blocks, or makes salient.

## Boundary policing
Discourse that excludes, narrows, or reclassifies candidate members of a category.

## Expanded argument
A representation in which the relevant empirical and normative premises are explicit.

## Compressed argument
A representation in which some inferential material is omitted but potentially recoverable from a classificatory concept or its conventional role.

## Classification event
An instance in which an entity, population, event, or territory is assigned to or denied membership in a category.

## Uptake
Social adoption or stabilization of an engineered conceptual usage.
