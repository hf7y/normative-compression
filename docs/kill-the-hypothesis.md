# Kill the Hypothesis

This file records observations that would seriously weaken or falsify the normative-compression account.

## Current kill conditions

1. No compression effect after reasonable controls for genre, length, discipline, and advocacy status.
2. Equivalent compression in neutral/descriptive classifications.
3. Boundary policing unrelated to preservation of downstream normative inference.
4. Independent annotators cannot reliably identify the observable components.
5. Proposed metrics cannot recover known compression levels in synthetic validation.
6. Alternative models explain the corpus with equal or greater predictive performance and fewer assumptions.
7. The phenomenon appears only after analyst-defined categories that already encode the hypothesis.

## Rule

A result that weakens the hypothesis should be documented here rather than treated as an inconvenience to be explained away.
