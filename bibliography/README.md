# Bibliography

The bibliography is a methodological dependency graph, not merely a reading list.

## Core literatures

### Conceptual engineering

Carnap; explication; Cappelen; Scharp; Haslanger; ameliorative analysis; conceptual replacement; conceptual introduction.

### Inferentialism and conceptual role

Work on inferential roles, conceptual contents, norms of assertion/inference, and the engineering of inferential consequences.

### Rorty and pragmatism

Rorty; contingency of vocabularies; irony; redescription; anti-foundationalism; pragmatism and political vocabulary.

### Implementation and uptake

Conceptual diffusion, stabilization, uptake, institutionalization, social control of language use.

### Formal and cognitive models

Information theory; minimum description length; graph theory; argument mining; predictive processing; causal models; computational semantics.

### Corpus-assisted discourse analysis

CADS; corpus design; balanced corpora; sampling; concordance; collocation; keyness; discourse prosody; reproducibility.

### Argument mining

Claim detection; premise/conclusion identification; argument graphs; relation extraction; annotation reliability.

### Indigenous studies and political theory

Competing theoretical accounts of indigeneity, territorial rights, sovereignty, settler colonialism, historical continuity, and migration/expansion.

## Literature-review questions

1. Has anyone already theorized normative compression under another name?
2. What existing inferentialist models can be operationalized?
3. What formal measures of argument complexity are already validated?
4. What corpus methods best support balanced sampling of scholarly discourse?
5. What conceptual-engineering literature treats uptake as deliberate social intervention?
6. What are the strongest objections to treating conceptual engineering as empirical engineering?
