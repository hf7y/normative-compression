# Interannotator Agreement

Agreement must be measured on independently annotated pilot material before scaling up.

## Candidate measures

- Cohen's kappa for two annotators and categorical labels;
- Krippendorff's alpha for multiple annotators/missing data;
- span overlap or token-level F1 for claim/criterion spans;
- graph-level agreement for argument relations.

## Procedure

1. Train annotators on a small shared set.
2. Annotate an independent pilot without discussion.
3. Measure agreement by field.
4. Inspect disagreement categories.
5. Revise codebook only through documented decisions.
6. Re-annotate a fresh sample after revision.

Low agreement on a theoretically central variable is a reason to revise or abandon that variable, not to adjudicate it into existence.
